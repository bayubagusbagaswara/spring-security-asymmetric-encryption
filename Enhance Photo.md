# Gabung Foto
```bash
Combine the uploaded photos into one high-quality vertical smartphone wallpaper collage.

Use all uploaded photos of the same person and arrange them into a clean, elegant, visually balanced collage suitable for a phone lock screen or home screen.

Preserve each original photo faithfully:
- Keep the same person, facial features, identity, expression, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions, pose, accessories, background, lighting direction, and camera angle.
- Do not alter or regenerate the person's face or body.
- Do not beautify excessively, reshape the body, slim the waist, enlarge or reduce body parts, or change skin tone.
- Preserve realistic skin texture, hair strands, shadows, and natural smartphone-photo characteristics.

Composition requirements:
- Create a vertical wallpaper with approximately a 9:16 or 9:19.5 aspect ratio.
- Make the person clearly visible and sufficiently large in every selected frame.
- Avoid making any person too small, stretched, distorted, cropped awkwardly, or disproportionately enlarged.
- Prioritize the strongest full-body or medium-full-body photo as the main image.
- Use the remaining photos as secondary panels around the main photo.
- Arrange the collage asymmetrically but neatly, like a premium lifestyle or fashion editorial collage.
- Keep important facial features, head, hands, torso, and feet visible whenever they are present in the original.
- Do not cover the person with another photo.
- Keep clean separation between images with subtle or minimal borders.
- Do not add decorative stickers, text, logos, frames, or graphic elements.
- Do not invent new poses or backgrounds.

Wallpaper usability:
- Leave enough visually calm space near the top area for phone clock and status icons.
- Keep the main subject away from areas where lock-screen UI would obstruct the face.
- Make the final composition feel spacious rather than overcrowded.
- Maintain a cohesive look by matching brightness, contrast, white balance, and sharpness across all images without changing the original scene.

Image enhancement:
- Improve sharpness, clarity, hair detail, skin detail, clothing texture, lighting balance, and overall resolution.
- Reduce blur, noise, compression artifacts, and grain.
- Keep the result natural, photorealistic, and faithful to the source images.
- Avoid over-sharpening, HDR effects, artificial skin smoothing, or AI-looking facial reconstruction.

Final output:
A polished, high-resolution vertical smartphone wallpaper collage using the uploaded photos, with the person remaining clear, realistic, proportional, recognizable, and consistent across every image.
```

# Kalau ingin format foto
```bash
Layout:
- Use one large vertical photo occupying most of the left side.
- Place one medium photo at the upper right.
- Place one larger vertical photo at the lower right.
- Add one smaller portrait/detail photo near the lower-left area if needed to balance the composition.
- Use slightly angled panel edges only if they improve the composition, but keep the result clean and modern.
- Do not crop faces, heads, hands, or feet unnecessarily.
```
